Hardware Power Meter Specification & Data Integrity

Compatible with split-core current transformer (CT) clamp meters (e.g., PZEM-004T, SCT-013) reporting on the measured_power_w telemetry channel. When physical CT meter readings are unavailable, AI Energy Guardian never substitutes simulated or modeled values.

Transparent Energy Guardian Measurement Taxonomy

AI Energy Guardian clearly distinguishes measured, forecast, calculated, and electrical power metrics. Physical hardware CT current meter is disconnected.

Measured (Hardware)

Real-time physical telemetry transmitted by classroom ESP32 microcontroller.

Forecast (ML Model)

Trained regression outputs predicting optimal fan and light counts.

Advisory (Rule Engine)

Rule-governed recommendations ensuring comfort and academic visibility.

Power (Electrical)

Derived from calibrated appliance wattage ratings. Never fabricated without transparency.
